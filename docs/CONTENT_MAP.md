## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Principles

The legacy Nuzul Tech website suffered from **single-page kitchen-sink overload**: 23 service cards, 3 overlapping pricing/hosting sections, repeated payment logos, duplicate case study feeds, competing floating support buttons, and simulated telemetry widgets stacked on a single scroll.

In the rebuilt architecture, the site operates simultaneously as:
1. **A High-Converting B2B Executive Landing Page (`/`)**: Strictly 8 focused sections ordered for conversion, with zero clutter, zero fake live telemetry, and clear pathways to deep technical evaluation.
2. **A Deep Multi-Page Corporate Website**: Dedicated routes for Services (`/services`, `/services/[slug]`), Pricing (`/pricing`), Cloud & Dedicated Hosting (`/hosting`), Case Studies (`/case-studies`, `/case-studies/[slug]`), Technical Insights Blog (`/blog`, `/blog/[slug]`), Consultation & Contact (`/contact`), and a custom 404 recovery page (`*`).

### Status Legend
- **`Home teaser`**: Concise summary component rendered on `/` that links to a dedicated inner route.
- **`Own page`**: Primary content section rendered on its dedicated inner route (`/services`, `/pricing`, `/hosting`, `/case-studies`, `/blog`, `/contact`, or `[slug]`).
- **`Global shell / Drawer`**: Persistent layout navigation, footer, or slide-over drawer used across routes.
- **`Merged duplicate`**: Legacy component whose unique content is merged into a cleaner canonical page/component so no Arabic copy is lost.
- **`Removed`**: Unverifiable telemetry widget, duplicate floating chat trigger, or obsolete modal superseded by real routes.
